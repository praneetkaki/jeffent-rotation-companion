# Flashcard front wording guide

Paste this into OpenEvidence (or any reviewer) along with a module's cards. Audience: ENT sub-interns.

## Rules for the front (question)
1. Ask a direct, answerable question. Never "What should a student know about X?", "Describe X", "Tell me about X", "Overview of X", or "Approach to X" with no stated task.
2. One task per card, matched to the answer: "How do you manage X?", "What is the next step in X?", "What is the first-line treatment for X?", "What is the most common cause of X?", "Which nerve ...?", "Name the N ...".
3. Make the question's verb match what the back actually delivers. If the back is a management list, ask "How do you manage ...". If it is a differential, ask "What is the differential for ...".
4. If the back covers several unrelated topics, split it into separate cards, one question each.
5. Prefer specific over vague: name the patient, setting, or finding when it changes the answer ("In a child with unilateral foul nasal discharge, what must you rule out?").
6. Short: ideally under 20 words. No hints inside the question that give the answer away.
7. No em dashes. No filler like "important", "key", "commonly tested".
8. Do not change the clinical meaning of the answer. Only reword the front, and flag any card whose back does not answer its front.

## Output format (one block per card)
- id:
- current front:
- new front:
- split needed? (yes/no, and proposed extra cards)
