A quick, complete ENT exam sequence

Ears

Inspect the pinna and periauricular area. Perform otoscopy of both canals and TMs using the largest comfortable speculum, bracing the hand on the cheek.

Nose

Anterior rhinoscopy with a speculum visualizes only the anterior third: septum, turbinates, mucosa, discharge, and polyps.

Oral cavity / oropharynx

Remove dentures. Inspect dentition, buccal mucosa, palate, and the ventral and lateral tongue and floor of mouth, the highest-risk oral-cancer sites.

Nasopharynx / hypopharynx / larynx

These subsites cannot be seen on routine exam and require flexible laryngoscopy.

Neck

Systematic palpation of nodal levels I to VI, thyroid, and the parotid and submandibular glands.

Cranial nerves

An itemized screen with left-right comparison. Emphasize CN VII in otologic disease and CN X in voice complaints.

High yield

Flex NPL: when to scope

  • Hoarseness or dysphonia lasting more than 2 to 4 weeks.
  • Dysphagia, odynophagia, or globus with red flags.
  • Stridor or any airway concern.
  • Neck mass, to scope the upper aerodigestive tract for a primary.

The ENT regions at a glance

Region

Ear

Key structures

Auricle and external auditory canal; tympanic membrane; ossicles; Eustachian tube; cochlea and vestibule.

High-yield pathologies

Otitis media and externa, cholesteatoma, hearing loss, vestibular disorders.

Region

Nose and sinuses

Key structures

Septum; turbinates and meatuses; frontal, ethmoid, maxillary and sphenoid sinuses.

High-yield pathologies

Rhinosinusitis, epistaxis, septal deviation, nasal polyps.

Region

Oral cavity and pharynx

Key structures

Tongue and floor of mouth; tonsils; palate; nasopharynx, oropharynx and hypopharynx.

High-yield pathologies

Tonsillitis, peritonsillar abscess, oral and oropharyngeal cancer.

Region

Larynx

Key structures

Epiglottis; thyroid and cricoid cartilages; vocal folds; recurrent laryngeal nerve.

High-yield pathologies

Laryngitis, vocal fold paralysis, laryngeal cancer, airway obstruction.

Region

Neck

Key structures

Anterior and posterior triangles; nodal levels I to VII; thyroid, parotid and submandibular glands.

High-yield pathologies

Malignant neck mass, thyroid nodule, deep neck infection.
